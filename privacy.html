Showy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Showy is a Mac app that runs on your own computer. The developer of Showy does not operate a server for it and does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

What stays on your Mac

Your slideshows, images, captions, research notes and schedule are saved in a folder on your Mac. They are not uploaded anywhere by Showy unless you choose one of the actions below.

If you connect a TikTok account

  • You sign in on TikTok's own website. Showy never sees your TikTok password.
  • TikTok gives Showy an access token and your display name. These are stored on your Mac, encrypted with the macOS keychain.
  • When you post, Showy sends the slides and caption you chose directly from your Mac to TikTok. Showy asks TikTok which audience options your account allows and for the status of posts you sent.
  • Showy does not read your TikTok videos, followers, messages or analytics.
  • To disconnect, remove the account in Showy, or revoke access in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.

Other services you may choose to use

  • Image search opens a Pinterest page from your Mac. Pinterest's own privacy policy applies to that visit.
  • AI images are made by OpenAI's Codex running on your Mac under your own sign-in. A reference image you choose is sent to OpenAI for that purpose.

No analytics, no ads, no selling

Showy has no analytics, tracking or advertising, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties by the developer.

Deleting your data

Delete the Showy folder on your Mac and remove the app. Nothing remains elsewhere under the developer's control.
