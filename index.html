Showy

TikTok photo slideshows, made on your Mac.

Showy is a small Mac app for making TikTok photo slideshows. You write the slides, pick or generate the images, export them at TikTok's size, and send them to your phone or to your own TikTok account.

What it does

  • Slide editor with TikTok-style text: outline, shadow or plain, any position.
  • Exports 1080 × 1920 images, or a short video of the slides.
  • A week calendar for planning posts.
  • Optional posting to your own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API.
  • Works with an AI agent on your Mac through a local connection.

Where your data lives

Everything you make stays in a folder on your Mac. Showy has no server and no account system.

Showy is an independent personal project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.
